# Listening content

The new mission scripts and their generated audio are CC BY 4.0, attributed to Vocab Drawer. Audio in missions, challenges, reading, literature, and sounds is synthetic speech generated with eSpeak NG 1.52, whose software is GPL-3.0-or-later. The synthesizer is not bundled with the app. Synthetic output is labelled in the UI. Reading scripts retain their app authorship; literature sources and source URLs are in src/data/lessons.js.

The six original human story recordings in [human/](human/) retain their individual public-domain or Creative Commons terms. See [human recording attribution](human/ATTRIBUTION.md), [integrity and provenance manifest](human/manifest.json), and original Commons metadata in human/sources.json. The recordings were copied unchanged; source byte counts and SHA-1 hashes were verified, and local SHA-256 hashes are recorded.

The existing 215 human word recordings retain their original speaker and CC BY-SA credits in src/data/voices.
